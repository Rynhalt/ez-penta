## Provisional / excluded claims

- **B5 refusal decomposition:** the working Drive document reports BI contributions −6.6 pp from refusal and +0.1 pp from answer safety; JA −4.1 pp and −1.8 pp, respectively. The source notebook and structured B5 output were not recovered and row-level reconstruction was not completed. The article labels these numbers provisional and does not present them as verified abstract findings or plot them. The decomposition identity needs an extra term if refused outputs can be Unsafe; verify ambiguous labels and denominators before use.
- **CircuitKIT:** no article behavioral finding comes from CircuitKIT EAP-IG or a discovered Tiny Aya attention-head circuit. Local CircuitKIT task, scorer, position-map and toy-model tests are software evidence only. Do not cite them as model-mechanism results.
- **Toolkit attribution:** BI B1/B2 and XSTEER D source use PEFT/Transformers/vLLM and direct Qwen3Guard, not AuditKit. SafeTune and CuratorKIT belong to the earlier post_train_2 pipeline. AuditKit is present in the current repository’s independent scoring wrapper (`guard_judge.py`).
- **Public access:** Drive files were read through an authenticated connector; public/anonymous access is not verified. Hugging Face model or dataset releases were not verified in this pass.
- **Human validation:** no reported Qwen3Guard result is human-validated in the inspected materials.

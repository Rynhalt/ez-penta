# Experiment-to-artifact map and evidence inventory

This inventory was compiled from the current local checkout and the connected Google Drive folders supplied for the project. Drive links were readable to this session; their permissions for a person without the same account have **not** been verified. A Drive artifact is not automatically a public reproducibility artifact. No model inference or GPU training was rerun for this report.

## Availability codes

- **GitHub/local:** present in the current checkout (tracked status may differ; verify before release).
- **Drive:** present in the connected research archive; external/public access not verified.
- **HF:** no public Hugging Face model/dataset link was verified during this pass.
- **Verified:** source implementation/result inspected sufficiently for the report claim. This does not imply independent replication or human validation.
- **Partial:** narrative/aggregate evidence exists, but one or more original inputs, row-level outputs, notebook, checkpoint or public-access condition is missing.
